Project 1

Navigating Hybrid Work Challenges in Building Automation: An Organizational Analysis and Strategic Roadmap for Somfy Systems

Developed for my Management Skills course, this paper examines how hybrid work dynamics and market volatility have decreased employee engagement and trust within the Sales and Distribution Department at Somfy Systems. The study analyzes how task-focused management and reactive workflows leave frontline workers feeling disconnected. To resolve this output problem, my team recommended increasing leadership transparency and implementing structured, biweekly goal-alignment check-ins modeled after systems applied by Intel and FedEx.
